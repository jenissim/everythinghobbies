About Everything Hobbies

A space for all the things that make you you.

We wanted a place where hobbies don't have to be productive, impressive, or profitable.

Maybe you read three books a month. Maybe you have not finished a book in two years but still love walking into a bookshop. Maybe you cook every day, collect playlists, save outfits you will never wear, write at 2am, or want to learn pottery next Tuesday.

That counts.

Our only rule

Be curious.
Be respectful.
Have fun.

Everything Hobbies is somewhere between a digital magazine, a community noticeboard and the group chat where everyone keeps sending recommendations.

Something for everyone.
Especially you.
