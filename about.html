A little about us

Life is better when there's something to talk about.

Everything Hobbies is a growing space for the things that make us curious, the interests that bring us joy, and the conversations that happen when we find people who love what we love.

Explore the website Discover the Book Club

01 / The idea

More than a hobby. A little piece of who you are.

We all have those things we can spend hours talking about. The book we cannot put down. The song we keep replaying. The subject we could explore all afternoon. The interest that makes an ordinary day feel a little more like our own.

Everything Hobbies was created around that feeling. We want to make room for curiosity, discovery and the simple pleasure of finding something you genuinely enjoy.

This is a place to explore your interests, find ideas worth spending time with, and discover conversations that might stay with you long after you have left the page.

Your interests deserve space. Your curiosity deserves to be fed.

What we believe

You don't have to be an expert to belong in the conversation.

You can be a beginner, a lifelong enthusiast, or someone looking for their next favourite thing. Come curious. Stay because something speaks to you.

03 / Our approach

Built around the things that make us, us.

We want Everything Hobbies to feel welcoming, thoughtful and genuinely enjoyable to return to. Not another place where you need to know everything before you begin.

As the website grows, we want its content and community to grow with it. The aim is to create something people can contribute to, discover through and make their own.

  • 01

    Curiosity first

    There is always something new to learn, try, read or discuss.

  • 02

    Room for everyone

    You do not need a perfect routine, specialist knowledge or a particular background to enjoy what interests you.

  • 03

    Meaningful conversations

    The best interests often become even better when they give us something to share with someone else.

  • 04

    Always evolving

    This is a growing project, shaped over time by new ideas, interests and the people who become part of it.
