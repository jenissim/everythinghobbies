About Everything Hobbies

A space for all
the things that
make you you.

For the things you love, the things you're curious about, and the things you haven't tried yet.

↓

The idea

Hobbies don't
have to mean
anything.

We wanted a place where the things you enjoy don't have to be productive, impressive, profitable or explainable.

Maybe you read three books a month. Maybe you haven't finished a book in two years but still love walking into a bookshop. Maybe you cook every day, collect playlists, save outfits you'll never wear, write at 2am, or decide you want to learn pottery next Tuesday.

That counts.

Our only rule

Be curious.
Be respectful.
Have fun.

That's really it.

Everything Hobbies is somewhere between a digital magazine, a community noticeboard and the group chat where everyone keeps sending recommendations.

There are no experts required here. No perfect taste. No right way to enjoy something. Just people finding things they like and talking about them.
