A little about us

More than just a reading list.

The Everything Hobbies Book Club is for people who love getting lost in a story, discovering a new perspective and sharing what a book made them feel or think about.

From fiction and contemporary literature to memoirs and unexpected discoveries, this is a space to read at your own pace and connect through the stories we choose.

Our reading list is still taking shape. We want the books we feature to be chosen with care, so the final selections will be shared here once they are ready.

How it works

Read. Reflect. Connect.

A book club should make reading more enjoyable, not feel like another deadline. Here is the idea behind ours.

01 / DISCOVER

Find your next read.

Explore the books selected for the club and discover stories you might not have picked up on your own.

02 / READ

Take your time.

Read at a pace that works for you. The point is to enjoy the experience and make room for the story.

03 / CONNECT

Share your thoughts.

Join the conversation, exchange perspectives and hear what other readers took away from the same book.
