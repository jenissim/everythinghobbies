Our selected reads · 01–04

The speculative read

01 / THE READING LIST

Science fiction · Dystopian

Parable of the Sower

Octavia E. Butler

Set in a society unravelled by inequality, violence and environmental collapse, this novel follows Lauren Olamina as she searches for a way to survive and imagines a different future. Butler's story explores belief, community, power and what it takes to keep going when the world around you changes.

Things to think about

  • What makes a community worth protecting?
  • Can a new belief system offer hope without becoming another form of control?
  • How does Lauren's understanding of change shape the choices she makes?

The romance read

02 / THE READING LIST

Romance · Contemporary fiction

Seven Days in June

Tia Williams

Eva Mercy and Shane Hall have a history that neither has quite managed to leave behind. Fifteen years after their intense teenage romance, the two writers find themselves unexpectedly reunited. What follows is a story about second chances, creative ambition, old wounds and the complicated ways people learn to love each other as adults.

Things to think about

  • Can two people reconnect without repeating the patterns that hurt them?
  • How do past experiences influence the way we receive love?
  • What is the difference between chemistry and compatibility?

The literary read

03 / THE READING LIST

African literature · Coming of age

Purple Hibiscus

Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie

In Nigeria, fifteen-year-old Kambili grows up in a home where silence and strict expectations shape everyday life. A visit to her aunt's household introduces her to a different kind of freedom. Through family, faith, political unrest and the search for a voice of her own, Adichie examines what happens when obedience and love become difficult to tell apart.

Things to think about

  • How does silence function as both protection and control?
  • What changes when Kambili encounters a different way of living?
  • How can love exist alongside fear, and what does that cost a family?

The South African read

04 / THE READING LIST

South African fiction · Social thriller

BARE: The Blesser's Game

Jackie Phamotse

Set against the pursuit of wealth, status and visibility in Johannesburg, this novel follows Treasure as she navigates a world where luxury comes with expectations and relationships can carry hidden costs. Phamotse explores ambition, exploitation, social pressure and the price of wanting a life that looks perfect from the outside.

Things to think about

  • What does the pursuit of status ask people to sacrifice?
  • How do money and power influence relationships?
  • Where should we draw the line between ambition and self-preservation?

Content note: explores exploitation, coercive relationships and difficult social realities.
